# WedPilot AI — Project Overview

## Vision

**WedPilot AI** transforms how luxury wedding planning agencies manage their operations. It provides an intelligent, AI-powered platform that goes beyond traditional wedding planning to deliver operational resilience, vendor intelligence, and real-time coordination.

## Tagline

> Beyond Wedding Planning. Intelligent Wedding Resilience.

## Target Users

- Luxury wedding planning agencies
- High-end wedding coordinators
- Premium event management companies

## Core Modules

### 1. Wedding Blueprint
Personalised wedding planning with task organisation, timeline management, and milestone tracking.

### 2. Vendor Intelligence
Smart vendor comparison, quotation analysis, performance tracking, and intelligent vendor selection.

### 3. Wedding Command Centre
Live wedding-day coordination with real-time schedules, team communication, and operational visibility.

### 4. Disruption & Recovery Intelligence
Proactive identification of operational disruptions with AI-powered recovery strategies and contingency planning.

### 5. Financial & Guest Intelligence
Budget management, payment tracking, guest list management, RSVP tracking, and financial analytics.

## Brand Identity

- **Feel:** Luxury, trust, intelligence, sophistication
- **Palette:** Plum & Antique Gold
- **Typography:** Editorial, professional (Cormorant Garamond + Inter)
- **Design Language:** Premium hospitality meets enterprise SaaS
